# Photometabolism

**In his words, light assisting electron transfer. Not photosynthesis.**

Near the end of the talk he coins the term. If polychromatic light is what mitochondria use best, then the sun is modulating metabolism. Photometabolism is the name for that assistance. Photosynthesis would mean the body producing energy from light. He says that is not the claim.

> "a new term that we've coined called photometabolism, not photosynthesis."

> "We're not producing energy. We're simply taking light from the sun and assisting in the electron transfer."

The opening clip of the video is the same sentence he returns to here: the sun is modulating metabolism. He attaches it to the seasonal pattern. Influenza, and deaths from heart disease, cancer, stroke, chronic lower respiratory disease, and Alzheimer's, rise and fall together. Peak one to three weeks after the shortest day. Nadir one to three weeks after the longest day. See [Seasons and Latitude](seasons.md).

## What the preprint is claiming to add

He shows a slide of papers. Some are about infrared. Some are about mitochondrial electron transfer. Some are about Marcus theory. Some show that infrared affects reorganization. None before theirs, he says, puts all of those into one model of electron transfer. That is the contribution he believes they have. The work is a preprint they are still trying to publish. He says you can download it. This wiki does not add a link he did not give.

He also says "the product" they are about to discuss is nothing new. The pages take that as his claim that the phenomenon is not new. The information was locked in separate fields: astrophysics, light engineering, and biology. It is not a description of a commercial product.

He tells this audience that looking at the Neuronic helmet is looking at electron transfer. That is an aside to the room. It is not a trial result.

# About

This wiki is a plain-language guide to one talk:

> Roger Seheult, MD. *When Light Meets Metabolism: Infrared Light, Mitochondria, and Electron Transfer.* Neuronic, 11 November 2026. 59 minutes. [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IF-77dkTkAU).

The YouTube title is "Infrared Light Can Speed Mitochondria Up, or Slow Them Down."

Seheult is introduced as a quadruple board certified physician in internal medicine, pulmonary disease, critical care, and sleep medicine, associate clinical professor at UC Riverside, assistant clinical professor at Loma Linda, and a co-founder of MedCram. He thanks Liam for the invitation. He names a preprint with Bob Fosbury (astrophysics), Scott Zimmerman (light engineering, Silas Incorporated), and Glenn Jeffery (biology, University College London). He says the group has not done the experimental data themselves. They read published work through Rudolph Marcus's electron-transfer model.

The pages report what the speakers say. They are not medical advice. The central claim is a preprint hypothesis.
